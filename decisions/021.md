## 021: Count SA LP pre-funded warrants as shares (2026-09-12)

**Status:** Final

**Decision:** Pre-funded warrants (exercise price $0.0001) count as shares throughout the SA LP copycat. The 2026-06-29 SHAZ layer uses the Form 3 filed that day (1,696,127 shares plus warrants for 6,374,823, total 8,070,950) instead of the 13G's 5,404,540, which a 19.99% ownership limit caps. Full 13Fs that omit known warrants are scaled by (shares + warrants) / shares, keeping the 13F's quarter-end price: the Q2 2026 SHAZ row (5,396,127 shares) adds the 2,674,823 warrants reported in the 2026-07-02 Form 4. The 2026-08-28 SHAZ 13D (8,070,950) then adds nothing and is omitted. This replaces decision 020's clause that used reported aggregates as filed.

**Rejected:**
- **Use each filing's reported aggregate (decision 020).** The same holding was counted three ways: capped in the 13G, shares only in the 13F, shares plus warrants in the 13D. That understated exposure by about 2.67 million shares from June 29 and invented a purchase on August 28, when the 13D reports no trades besides the June 30 exercise.
- **Count ordinary shares only.** Consistent with the 13F convention, but understates the fund's economic exposure by about 2.7 million shares after June 30 and more before.

**Evidence:** The Q2 2026 13F information table lists SHAZ as 5,396,127 SH worth $456,836,112 with no warrant row. A free-data run on 2026-09-12 gave SHAZ 8,070,950 shares in the June 29 layer and $683.3M in the adjusted Q2 2026 13F. Chosen by Pablo after the inconsistency was explained.

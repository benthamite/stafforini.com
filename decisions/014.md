## 014: Keep the SA LP copycat disclosure-based after forced sales (2026-08-15)

**Decision:** The copycat model continues to follow public SEC disclosures when press reports describe a later forced liquidation. Add a dated caveat about the stale snapshot, but do not reconstruct an undisclosed current portfolio.

**Rejected:**
- **Treat the reported liquidation as a new rebalance.** Reports disagreed on whether the fund sold most or all public equities, and did not disclose a complete trade list, quantities, or prices.
- **Infer the remaining book from later issuer-specific filings.** An August 4 Schedule 13D/A proved that Core Scientific shares remained, but it covered only one issuer and could not identify the full portfolio.
- **Describe the June 30 13F as current on August 14.** The filing predates the reported July margin call by several weeks and would mislead readers without a caveat.

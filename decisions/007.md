## 007: Duplicate work pages get a canonical, not a deletion or a noindex (2026-07-28)

**Decision:** When two cite keys render byte-identical work pages, keep both pages, give the redundant one a `canonical` param naming the page it duplicates, and exclude it from `sitemap.xml`. It stays crawlable and is deliberately **not** noindexed. `assign_duplicate_canonicals` in `scripts/generate-work-pages.py` detects the groups by hashing rendered page content; `_duplicate_primary_slug` picks the survivor (the undecorated slug beats a trailing disambiguator letter; otherwise the longest, most descriptive slug wins). 66 pairs on 2026-07-28.

**Rejected:**
- **Delete the redundant bib entry.** The obvious "fix the source, not the symptom" move, and wrong here. 54 of the 66 redundant entries live in `~/My Drive/repos/babel-refs` — a *shared* bibliography, so removal is someone else's decision, not this site's. 7 of the 66 keys are also cited from `~/My Drive/notes` or `bibliographic-notes`, and a naive "keep the shorter slug" rule would have deleted `Godwin1793EnquiryConcerningPolitical` (cited) in favour of a bare `Godwin1793`. Deduplicating the bibliography is a real task, but it is a bibliography task requiring per-entry judgement, not an indexing one.
- **`noindex` the duplicate** (via the existing `unlisted` param, which `is-indexable.html` already honours). Contradicts the canonical signal — a page that says "the real version is over there" should not also say "do not index me". Google's guidance for duplicates is a canonical, and a noindexed page cannot pass its signals to the canonical target.
- **Leave it to Google.** This is the status quo that produced the problem: both URLs were in the sitemap, Google picked a winner itself, and filed the loser under "Duplicate, Google chose different canonical than user" (131 URLs). Naming the winner is the whole point.

**Consequence:** `head.html` prefers `.Params.canonical` over `.Permalink`, and `sitemap.xml` skips any page with `canonical` set — a page that points elsewhere must not be advertised in the sitemap.

**Files:** `scripts/generate-work-pages.py`, `layouts/partials/head.html`, `layouts/_default/sitemap.xml`, `tests/test_generate_work_pages.py`.

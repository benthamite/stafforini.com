## 020: Layer every newer Schedule 13D/13G onto the SA LP copycat timeline (2026-09-12)

**Status:** Final

**Decision:** From its filing date, each Schedule 13D, 13D/A or 13G whose holdings date is later than the quarter end of the latest full 13F sets that issuer's long position to the aggregate beneficial ownership the schedule reports. Layers accumulate on the previous portfolio until the next full 13F replaces it. A schedule whose holdings are no newer than an already-public 13F is omitted, and the model raises if one is listed or if two disclosures share a filing date. Section 16 Forms 3/4 are not layered. Reported aggregates are used as filed, so SHAZ positions include pre-funded warrants and the June 2026 13G figure is capped at 19.99%. This replaces the single hard-coded Nebius 13G layer.

**Rejected:**
- **Keep only the Nebius 13G.** The note's own rule said later issuer disclosures update the portfolio, yet the SHAZ 13G and three CORZ 13Ds were silently ignored.
- **Count economic exposure from Forms 3/4.** Adding pre-funded warrants disclosed on Form 3 would raise SHAZ on 2026-06-29 above the 13G's capped figure, but it mixes forms and adds a judgment the schedules do not make.
- **Layer each schedule directly on its base 13F.** A later layer would drop earlier issuer updates since that 13F.

**Evidence:** A free-data run of `sa-data`/`sa-lib` on 2026-09-12 produced six layers (CORZ 2025-08-19 and 2025-10-14; NBIS 2026-05-27; SHAZ 2026-06-29; CORZ 2026-08-04; SHAZ 2026-08-28), each accumulating correctly and resetting at the next 13F. The SHAZ 13G/A of 2026-08-14 (as of June 30) is omitted because the Q2 2026 13F followed 19 minutes later.
